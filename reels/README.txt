HOW TO ADD YOUR REELS:
---------------------
1. Copy or move your 9:16 vertical MP4 video files into this "reels" folder.
   (For example: reels/reel1.mp4, reels/reel2.mp4, or any name like reels/our_trip.mp4).

2. Open "reels/reels.json" and add your reel details:
   [
     {
       "title": "Your Title Here",
       "src": "reels/your_video_file.mp4",
       "tag": "Special Memory"
     }
   ]

3. That's it! In the Aura Cinema "Added Videos" dropdown, select "Our Reels", and your videos will play in full 9:16 vertical format with smooth 1:1 finger swipe physics (swipe up/down like Instagram Reels, single tap to play/pause, sound toggle pill).
